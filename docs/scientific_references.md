# Scientific references

Sources named in the challenge brief. The reference ranges used in `src/utils/constants.py` are general adult values
for demonstration. **Before real use they must be reviewed against NASA Standard 3001 and by a flight surgeon.**

## Spaceflight hazards and risks

- NASA Human Research Program, *5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight*: radiation, isolation and confinement, distance from Earth,
  gravity fields, hostile closed environments.
- NASA Human Research Program, *Risks of Human Spaceflight*.
- NASA Human Research Program, ongoing research in space.

## Partner agencies

- Canadian Space Agency, *Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health*.
- Canadian Space Agency, *Why Do We Conduct Science Experiments in Space?*

## How each indicator maps to the material

| Indicator | Hazard link | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Heart rate, blood pressure | Gravity fields, distance | Fluid shifts and cardiovascular deconditioning |
| Glucose | Distance, isolation | Stress, activity and nutrition |
| Temperature | Radiation, closed environment | Immune changes and infection spread |
| Sleep, mood | Isolation and confinement, radiation | Behavioral health |
| Oxygen saturation | Hostile closed environment | Cabin air quality |
| Exercise | Gravity fields | Bone and muscle loss countermeasure |
