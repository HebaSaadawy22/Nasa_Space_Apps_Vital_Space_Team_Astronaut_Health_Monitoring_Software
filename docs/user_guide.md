# User guide

## Before you start

Pick a crew member and an environment in the sidebar. The environment changes the escalation advice, not the numbers.

## Reading the status colors

| Color | Word | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Green | Normal | Within the reference range |
| Amber | Watch | Outside the normal range. Measure again and mention it at the next medical check-in |
| Red | Alert | Follow the actions and contact the flight surgeon as the banner says |

Color is always shown together with a word.

## Daily routine

1. Open **Health Dashboard** and measure each indicator. Each indicator has a *How to measure* note.
2. Enter the values in **Log today's readings** and save. This adds the next mission day.
3. Read the banner and the guidance for any amber or red indicator.
4. Open **Health Analytics** to see whether a value is drifting from your own baseline.
5. Tick off your tasks on **Daily Routine**.

## Other pages

- **Interactive Body**: click a marker to see what that system does, what spaceflight does to it and the countermeasure.
- **Space Hazards**: the five NASA hazards, each with a score and the indicators that watch it.
- **Health Assistant**: ask in plain words, for example "How am I doing?" or "Why is my sleep low?".

## Limits

Reference ranges are general adult values for demonstration. This tool does not diagnose and is not medical advice.
